FAQ

Questions before you begin.

What is BIYE?

BIYE is a Marriage Intelligence Platform designed to help people understand meaningful compatibility—not simply discover profiles.

Does AI decide who I should marry?

No. The deterministic Rule Engine owns official compatibility scoring. AI is assistive for explanation, synthesis and guidance. The final decision remains human.

Why 137+?

BIYE currently represents 137 compatibility metrics, while the underlying knowledge graph is designed to expand beyond that number.

Is 100% compatibility a guarantee?

No. Compatibility is information for better understanding and discussion, not a guarantee of relationship success.

Is health/genetic information public?

No. It is intended to be private, consent-controlled, awareness-oriented and non-diagnostic. It must not automatically label someone unsuitable for marriage.

Are future-life images predictions?

No. Couple Studio and future-age visualizations are illustrative and should never be treated as factual predictions.
